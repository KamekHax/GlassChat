Service status

GlassChat is a limited Beta text-chat project. The public central service is not yet deployed. The current source includes GlassChat-owned accounts and a single seeded Commons/#general text channel; direct messages, voice, video, screen sharing, attachments, account deletion and moderation/reporting controls are not implemented.

Beta channel message text is stored centrally as readable plaintext at the application layer. The service is not local-first and does not provide end-to-end encryption. Do not use it for private or sensitive conversations.

Accounts and acceptable use

Accounts require an email address and password. Users should keep credentials and one-time links private. The detailed conduct and service-use rules are proposed in the Community Guidelines and Acceptable Use Policy drafts. These policies are not yet final and the product does not currently provide complete user reporting or appeals tools.

Availability, content and account closure

No uptime, support response, data-retention, backup or recovery guarantee is established. The operator has not implemented self-service account deletion or specified how account/content removal requests will be fulfilled. These terms do not promise service availability, preservation of messages or an account deletion outcome.

Any user-content license, intellectual-property allocation, suspension/termination process, limitation of liability, dispute procedure, governing law and effective date require operator and counsel review; this draft does not invent those terms.
