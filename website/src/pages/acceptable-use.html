Acceptable Use Policy.

This draft proposes basic service-use boundaries. It does not create an enforcement program that the current Beta does not implement.

Do not use GlassChat to

  • Access accounts, systems, communities or data without authorization.
  • Send malware, phishing or credential-theft attempts, or exploit service vulnerabilities.
  • Interfere with the availability, integrity or security of GlassChat or another person's systems.
  • Harass, threaten, stalk, impersonate or expose private information about another person.
  • Send unlawful material, spam, automated bulk messages or deceptive links.
  • Attempt to bypass authentication, rate limits, community membership or other access controls.

What the product currently supports

The source Beta is limited to authenticated text chat in one seeded community/channel. It does not implement bots, public webhooks, community administration, blocking, reporting, moderation queues or an appeals flow. Do not assume a prohibited action can be automatically detected or acted upon.

Security and reports

For ordinary bugs, use the support page. For suspected vulnerabilities, follow the security reporting instructions. Do not include passwords, access tokens, reset links or another user's message content in public reports.

For support, privacy requests, security reports and legal correspondence, contact kamekfnbr@gmail.com.

Owner review

Before this policy is used as a condition of service, the operator and counsel must define its scope, prohibited content categories, reporting and response steps, enforcement decisions, appeals and relationship to applicable law. No suspension, removal or response-time guarantee is made by this draft.
