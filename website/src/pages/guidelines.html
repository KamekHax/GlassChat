Community Guidelines.

These proposed principles are for review and are not yet an implemented or fully enforceable moderation program.

How members should treat one another

  • Communicate respectfully; do not threaten, target, stalk or harass other members.
  • Do not share another person's private information without permission.
  • Do not post illegal content, malware, credential-stealing links or instructions intended to compromise other people or services.
  • Do not spam, impersonate another person, or use GlassChat to disrupt a community.
  • Keep passwords, verification links, reset tokens and session details private.

Current product limitations

The Beta source seeds the Commons community and one #general channel. User-facing community creation, roles, moderation tools, member blocking, reporting, message removal and appeals are not implemented. No moderator staffing or response time has been promised. Do not rely on these draft guidelines as a substitute for missing safety controls.

Raise a concern

For ordinary product support, use the support page. For a suspected vulnerability, use the private contact instructions on the security page; do not post exploit details or sensitive information publicly. The product does not yet have an in-app report/appeal workflow.

For support, privacy requests, security reports and legal correspondence, contact kamekfnbr@gmail.com.

Owner review

Before opening a community, the operator must decide which conduct rules are binding, how reports are received and reviewed, what enforcement options exist, whether appeals are available, and what user-protection requirements apply. Do not claim a rule is actively enforced until the service supports it.
